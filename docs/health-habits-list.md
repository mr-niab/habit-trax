# Health and Wellbeing Habits to Track

A short list of research-backed habits that are practical to log in a personal habit tracker.

- **No food within 3 hours of sleep** — Later eating is associated with poorer sleep quality and lower sleep efficiency.[cite:3][cite:7][cite:13]
- **No nighttime snacking after dinner** — Nighttime eating patterns are linked with worse sleep efficiency and poorer sleep timing.[cite:3][cite:7]
- **Eat fruit or vegetables with each main meal** — Higher long-term fruit and vegetable intake is associated with lower all-cause mortality.[cite:18]
- **Get at least 30 minutes of moderate activity today** — WHO recommends adults aim for at least 150–300 minutes of moderate physical activity per week.[cite:17][cite:27]
- **Complete 2 or more strength sessions per week** — WHO recommends regular muscle-strengthening activity, and meta-analysis evidence links it with lower mortality and disease risk.[cite:17][cite:19]
- **Break up long sitting periods** — WHO recommends reducing sedentary behaviour because too much sitting is harmful to health.[cite:27][cite:30]
- **Avoid skipping breakfast** — Later and more irregular eating patterns, including breakfast skipping, are associated with worse sleep-related outcomes.[cite:3]
- **Hit your weekly cardio target** — A simple weekly goal is 150+ minutes of moderate activity or 75+ minutes of vigorous activity.[cite:17][cite:27]

## Simple tracking format

For easy logging, use:

- **Yes/No** for: no food before bed, no nighttime snacking, breakfast eaten, strength session completed, broke up sitting time.
- **Count** for: meals that included fruit or vegetables.
- **Minutes** for: daily activity and weekly cardio total.
